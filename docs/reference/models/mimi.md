# Mimi (neural audio codec, component)

The Mimi/Moshi-style 12 Hz neural audio codec (SEANet + split-RVQ,
encode and decode) [Qwen3-TTS](qwen3tts.md) decodes speech through. Not
independently servable: it has no capability manifest or CLI verb of its
own, reached only as Qwen3-TTS's Code2Wav stage.

Package: `brain-mimi`.
