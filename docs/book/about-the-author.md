# About the Author

Martin Schröder is an engineer and the founder of Swedish Embedded AB, a
Swedish engineering company that builds embedded systems, machine learning
systems and the software infrastructure that connects them. He designed
brain, the engine whose construction this book follows, and leads its
development.

His work sits where machine learning meets real hardware: making models run
correctly and efficiently on the devices people actually have, from data
centre GPUs to small accelerators, and building the tests and measurements
that show they do. The habits this book asks of its reader (derive before
you trust, measure before you claim, check every result against an
independent computation) are the habits that work has demanded.

He welcomes questions about the material, reports of errors and
conversations about difficult engineering problems at
[martin.schroder@swedishembedded.com](mailto:martin.schroder@swedishembedded.com).
More about the company and its work is at
